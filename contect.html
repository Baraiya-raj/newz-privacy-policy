About Newz: Bhavnagar Local News

Newz: Bhavnagar Local News (Newz – ભાવનગર ગુજરાતી સમાચાર) is an independent news aggregator and local news platform designed to provide timely updates from Bhavnagar and across Gujarat.

News Sources & Editorial Policy

Our app aggregates publicly available news updates and RSS feeds from reputable regional and national news outlets, official government press releases, and verified local news portals. We do not claim ownership of third-party news articles; full credit and original source links are provided with the respective news items.

Corrections & Contact

If you have any questions, feedback, copyright concerns, or wish to report an inaccuracy in any news item, please contact our editorial support team at baraiyaraj3000@gmail.com. We review all requests promptly.

We only collect information necessary to run the app, deliver local news, display ads, and maintain app stability:

  • Account & Profile Data (If Enabled): Name, email address, or phone number only when you sign in or create a profile.

8. Your Rights, Account Deletion & Data Deletion

  • Access & Correction: You may review or update available profile or local news preference settings directly in the app.

How to Request Deletion of Specific Data (Without Deleting Your Account):

  1. If the feature is available in the app, remove specific submitted content or update/clear your preferences inside the app settings.
  2. Otherwise, send an email to baraiyaraj3000@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Newz: Bhavnagar Local News".
  3. Include your registered email address, phone number, or user ID (if applicable) and clearly specify which data you want deleted (such as submitted user content or profile/preference data) without deleting your account.
  4. We acknowledge requests within 24 to 48 hours and complete verified deletion requests promptly (typically within a few business days, subject to backup overwrite cycles).

How to Request Full Account and Associated Data Deletion:

  1. Use the in-app account deletion option (if account sign-in is enabled in your app version), or email baraiyaraj3000@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Newz: Bhavnagar Local News" and your account identifier.
  2. Once verified, your account and associated profile/user-submitted records are scheduled for permanent deletion.

Data Deleted vs. Data Kept & Retention Period:

  • Data Deleted Upon Request: Account/profile details (name, email, or phone number, if sign-in was used), saved preferences, and user-submitted content/reports covered by the request are deleted from active application storage. Encrypted/archived backups are overwritten automatically on standard backup rotation cycles.
  • Data Kept & Retention Period: Information strictly required for legal or regulatory compliance (such as under the DPDP Act, 2023), fraud/spam/abuse prevention, or dispute/moderation enforcement may be retained as permitted by law. Aggregated analytics, advertising identifiers, and crash/diagnostic logs processed by third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob and Firebase) are governed by Google's retention schedules and privacy policies.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Welcome to Newz App (package name: com.bhavnagar.gujrat.localnews). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information in compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India), Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR, and COPPA.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to run the app, deliver local news, display ads, and maintain app stability:

  • Account & Profile Data (If Enabled): Name, email address, or phone number only when you sign in or create a profile.
  • Location Preferences: District, taluka, city, or area selection chosen by you (or approximate location if explicitly permitted) to show relevant local news.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Headlines, text, images, videos, or reports submitted by users, if submission features are enabled.
  • Device, Advertising & Diagnostic Data: Advertising ID, device model, OS version, app interactions, IP address, and crash/performance logs via Google AdMob and Firebase.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display location-relevant local news and updates.

Newz App is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent under local law). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us immediately so we can delete it.
